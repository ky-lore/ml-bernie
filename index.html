Our Story

The original name in California tire supply.

Founded in the 1940s, M. L. Bernie Co. was California's first — and remains its oldest — tire shop supply distributor. It's personal here: our family worked this business for more than 40 years before taking ownership, and we run it the same way our customers build their own shops — by showing up, keeping our word, and stocking what the job actually needs.
